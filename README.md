# T3x Dominion

A readable hex war from Futuret3Ch. Same fight as before — Energy, Data, Scout, Defender, Attacker, Artillery — but the board shows what you can do.

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser, or play it at [t3x.futuret3ch.com.au/play/dominion](https://t3x.futuret3ch.com.au/play/dominion).

## How to play

1. Bright hexes are legal moves. Red hexes are shots. Gold hexes are where a new unit can stand.
2. Energy builds units. Data only repairs. Each unit gets one action a turn: move, shoot, or repair.
3. Step onto a hex you do not own and it becomes yours. Shooting a unit hurts it; it does not take the land.
4. Hold 31 of 61 hexes, or wipe the enemy off the map.

No account and no server. The whole game is this page.
